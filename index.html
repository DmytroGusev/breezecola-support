Breeze Cola

Italian Briscola, with a little sunshine. An independent card game for iPhone and iPad.

Three cards. One trump.

Play solo at three computer levels, or meet friends through Apple Game Center. Two players or four in opposite partnerships. Original Italian suits, an optional French symbol deck, hints, undo, last trick, detailed scores and automatic offline save.

Italian, English, Russian, Ukrainian, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese. No advertising, purchases or tracking.

How to play

01 · Three cards each

40 cards in four suits: coins, cups, swords and batons. Two players, or four in opposite partnerships. The dealer deals three cards each and turns the trump card face up under the stock. The player to the dealer’s right leads; play proceeds counterclockwise.

02 · Play any card

You may play any held card. You never have to follow suit or trump. Highest trump wins; without a trump, highest card of the led suit wins. Order: A, 3, R (King), C (Knight), F (Knave), 7, 6, 5, 4, 2.

03 · Winner draws first

After each trick everyone draws one card, starting with the winner and proceeding counterclockwise. The face-up trump is drawn last. The winner leads the next trick. When the stock is empty, play the final three cards without drawing.

04 · Count points, not tricks

Ace: 11. Three: 10. King: 4. Knight: 3. Knave: 2. All other cards: 0. The deck contains 120 points. Partners share their captured points.

05 · Sixty-one wins

Count all cards at the end of the deal. At least 61 points wins. A 60–60 tie awards no win. First side to the selected number of won deals (1, 2 or 3) wins the match. The dealer rotates each deal.

06 · A table with friends

Game Center needs 2 or 4 distinct accounts, matching app versions and settings. Invite friends or find players. The host device manages the game; other players receive only their own hands. A disconnect stops play. No host migration, recovery after app termination or replacement bots. Hints and undo are offline only.

Support

For a bug or question, open a support issue. Include your app version, device model and steps to reproduce. Please do not include private information. For a damaged save, you can start a new game from the home screen. Game Center needs distinct signed-in accounts and matching game settings.

Deleting the app removes local progress. Online matches stop if a player leaves; host migration, replacement bots and recovery after termination are not supported.
