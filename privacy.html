Privacy policy

Breeze Cola is developed by Dmytro Husiev. We do not operate a gameplay server or collect personal data, analytics or advertising identifiers.

On your device

Your offline game, settings and statistics are stored locally using app storage and UserDefaults. They are not sent to the developer. Delete the app to remove its local data; device backup behavior is controlled by Apple and your settings.

Optional Game Center

Apple Game Center authenticates players and connects multiplayer matches. During play, connected participants receive Game Center display names and public game state. The host device holds the full deal and validates actions; guests receive only their own hands. This data is used transiently to run the match. Apple processes Game Center data under Apple’s privacy policy. Offline play does not require Game Center.

No tracking or advertising

The app includes no advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, in-app purchases, developer account or chat. We do not sell or share personal data. It is not designed specifically for children and does not ask users for their age or contact details.

Support and this website

If you voluntarily submit a GitHub support issue, GitHub processes and publishes that content under its policies. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary web request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. This website has no analytics scripts or cookies added by us.

Questions

Use the support page for privacy questions. Do not publish private information in a public issue.
